Permissions & your control

Understand the access.
Stay in control.

Android permissions support the features you choose to use. You can stop the reader and manage permissions in Android Settings.

Accessibility & screen reading

With your permission, SRA reads relevant ride-screen text to identify fares and distances. Screen capture and OCR can help where text is unavailable.

The Android app must explain this access and obtain your affirmative consent before you enable it. This website does not replace that in-app consent.

Overlay & live-service notices

Display-over-other-apps access shows compact advice while you review a ride. Android screen-sharing and foreground-service controls show when the live service is active.

Use Stop in SRA, stop screen sharing through Android, or revoke the relevant permission in Settings.

Optional location

The optional activity-location feature requires a separate choice in SRA and Android location permission. Location samples can be recorded during an active session when enabled.

You can keep this feature disabled. Location is not permission to make offers or accept rides for you.

Help & support

A clearer start.
A little help when needed.

Setup, calculation and account information in one place.

How do I get started?

Enter your vehicle’s fuel average, petrol price and preferred minimum and target rates in SRA. Read the screen-reader explanation before granting permissions. Use Start when you want live advice.

Why does pickup distance matter?

You drive the pickup distance before the passenger trip begins. Including both distances gives a fuller picture of the fare per kilometre you drive.

Is the effective rate my net profit?

No. Effective gross rate is fare divided by total driving distance. Fuel, commission, maintenance and other running costs affect your net earnings.

What if a distance is missing or incorrect?

Check the values against the ride screen. Correct them manually or try a clearer screenshot before relying on the calculation.

Does SRA accept a ride or enter an offer?

No. SRA provides guidance. You decide whether to accept a ride and enter every offer yourself in inDrive.

Privacy Policy

Smart Ride Advisor · Last updated 15 October 2026

Pre-release policy draft
Final retention periods and deletion handling must be confirmed before this policy is used for Google Play submission.

1. Who operates SRA?

Smart Ride Advisor is owned by Muhammad Waqas. It is an independent Android decision assistant for ride-hailing drivers in Pakistan and is not affiliated with inDrive.

2. Information handled by the Android app

SRA can handle account identifiers, sign-in details through authentication services, name, phone or email, city, working areas, vehicle details, fuel average, petrol price and preferred calculation settings.

Calculation records can include pickup and passenger trip distances, fares, effective rates, advice, the settings used and timestamps.

Device and service records can include device model, Android and app versions, login history, service sessions and manually selected activity phases.

If you separately enable the optional location feature and grant Android permission, location samples and derived activity or area information can be recorded during active sessions.

3. Why this information is used

These records support account access, saved preferences, ride calculations, calculation history, service activity and support. Authorized SRA administrators can access relevant records to operate and support the service. Any additional use in the released app must be disclosed before release.

4. Accessibility, screen capture and screenshots

With your permission, SRA reads relevant text exposed on ride screens through Android accessibility. Screen capture and OCR can identify ride values where supported, and screenshot checks process images you select.

Extracted fare and distance values may be saved in calculation history. SRA does not use this access to accept rides or enter offers for you. You can stop live reading or revoke its Android permissions. Accessibility use needs a separate in-app explanation and affirmative consent.

5. Service providers and data access

The Android project uses Google Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database and includes Firebase Analytics. Google services can process account, device and usage information according to the features and SDK configuration enabled in the app. Read Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google services.

The final policy and Google Play Data safety declaration must reflect the Android version that is actually released. No claim that SRA collects no data is made.

6. Retention and deletion

Account, profile, settings, calculation and service activity records may remain stored until removed. Final retention periods, backup handling and any lawful retention exceptions have not yet been published.

The current app implementation marks raw location samples for expiry after 30 days and attempts cleanup during service operation. This does not guarantee deletion at an exact time; cleanup and retained summaries need release verification.

Account deletion must address the sign-in account and its associated data, subject to any clearly disclosed retention obligations. You can request deletion by email using the account deletion section. Ownership verification, completion time and any retention exceptions will be explained when your request is reviewed.

7. Security and your choices

Use Android Settings to control permissions and SRA’s controls to stop live reading. Do not share passwords or verification codes. Production access controls, secure data handling and the final retention/deletion process must be verified before release.

8. This website

The example calculator runs locally in your browser and does not submit its entries to SRA. This website has no registration, payment or message-submission forms. Support and deletion links open your email app; you choose whether to send the message.

If you email support, your email address and message are used to respond and, where needed, verify account ownership. The hosting provider may process technical request information needed to serve the site.

9. Privacy contact and updates

The operator is Muhammad Waqas, Smart Ride Advisor. For privacy questions or data requests, email smartrideadvisor.support@gmail.com. The date above will be updated when this policy changes.
