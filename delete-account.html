Delete your account

Sign in with your email and password, then review the deletion warning and confirm to permanently remove your account and associated SRA data.

If your account uses phone-only sign-in, delete it from the signed-in Android app or contact support. This page does not send SMS codes.

This permanently deletes your data

Your Firebase sign-in account and the SRA profile and ride calculation records associated with it will be deleted. This cannot be undone. Deleting your SRA account does not affect your inDrive account.
